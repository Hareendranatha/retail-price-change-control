# Portfolio presentation

## Repository description
Google Forms, Sheets and Apps Script workflow for retail price change requests, VAT cost normalization, supplier margin review, decision tracking and operational reporting.

## Case study narrative
Retail price revisions were requested through phone calls and WhatsApp, creating a fragmented queue and repeated manual calculations. I designed a centralized workflow to capture requests, distinguish invoice cost formats, calculate comparable cost and retail spread, and support review against item-level supplier agreements. Shared decision statuses and prefilled requester messages make the process easier to coordinate, while reporting highlights volume and exceptions by branch, supplier, category and requester.

The portfolio implementation documents role controls, calculation assumptions, setup steps and test cases. Business impact should be evaluated using completion time, backlog and rework measures after deployment.
